Privacy Policy

Free & Clear is a service of Lifeofmorr LLC, Fort Lauderdale, Florida. Last updated October 2026.

What this website collects

This website is a static information page. It runs no advertising trackers and sells no data. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and pages requested) for security and operation, as any host does.

Payments

Checkout is handled by Stripe. When you buy, Stripe collects your payment information under Stripe's own privacy policy. We never see or store your full card number, and we never touch client money — our services are flat fees for defined deliverables, paid through Stripe.

If you become a client

To build your plan, you voluntarily provide documents — credit reports, account statements, and household figures — plus your contact details. That information is used for one purpose: building, delivering, and reviewing your family's plan. Client households are kept in our internal client record system so your ledger, chosen plan, milestones, and reviews stay together.

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with marketers.
  • We do not report anything to credit bureaus, creditors, or lenders on your behalf.
  • Where account-connection features are offered, they use provider-hosted sign-in flows — your bank credentials never pass through or get stored by Free & Clear; only the resulting account data (balances, rates, minimums, due dates) is used in your plan.
  • Anonymized, aggregated figures (never names or documents) may be used to describe typical results, and only in ways that cannot identify a family.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time what information we hold about your household, to correct it, or to delete it. Email don@lifeofmorr.com and we will act on the request promptly. Deleting your client record ends our ability to run monthly reviews against your plan.

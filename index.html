The problem, named the way you feel it

  • You make good money. So why does it feel like you're running in place?
  • You've got cards, a couple of "interest-free" plans, a buy-now-pay-later habit split into a dozen tiny loans, a car that's worth less than the loan — and no single page anywhere that adds it all up.
  • You've never seen the real total. Neither has your partner. That's not a character flaw — it's a missing system.
  • Meanwhile the minimums-only path quietly costs the average household we model tens of thousands in interest — and never actually ends.

The traps hide in plain sight. Our intake hunts them on purpose:

BNPL clusters

Buy-now-pay-later loans split into a dozen tiny plans — easy to forget, all still due.

Deferred-interest cliffs

"Interest-free" promos that charge every cent of back interest if a balance survives the promo end date.

Debts that aren't yours

Authorized-user accounts, old charge-offs, errors — listed on your report, not yours to pay.

Minimums only

In our founding household's model, paying only minimums never actually ends — balances still alive a decade out, and $70,000+ in interest paid along the way. The total payment also quietly shrinks as small balances die, which is exactly how the timeline stretches forever.

One payment, held steady

The same household on a single fixed monthly payment — every spare dollar aimed at the highest-rate target, each dead debt's payment rolling into the next — is debt-free in 28 months at roughly $19,600 of interest. Same income. Same debts. The difference is the system.

Who this is for — and who it isn't

For: two-income households, $100K+ income, $50K+ in mixed debt, tired of guessing.

Not for: anyone looking for a quick fix, a settlement mill, or someone to "make it go away." We don't do that. We build the map — you drive it, and it works.

The house rules every family runs on

  1. One war payment, every month, never stepping down.
  2. Autopay everything; a late fee is a plan failure, not a life event.
  3. No new debt until free — no cards, no BNPL, no exceptions.
  4. Lump sums go 100% to the current target the day they land.
  5. Fixed lines (tax plans, family loans, locked terms) stay current and untouched.
  6. Never sell a vehicle underwater or roll negative equity. Break-even first, decisions second.
  7. Removals are tracked, not paid. Prove deletion on the re-pull.
  8. Review monthly, adjust calmly, never abandon.

FAQ

Is this financial advice?

No — it's budgeting and planning education built from your documents. Tax, legal, or bankruptcy questions get referred to licensed professionals.

Do you contact my creditors or take over payments?

Never. Your money never touches us.

Will this fix my credit score?

We don't sell credit repair. Families usually see scores improve as utilization falls and errors get removed through proper channels — but the product is the plan, not a score promise.

What do you need from us?

Both partners' credit reports (we show you how to pull them), recent statements, and one honest conversation.

What if some of it isn't our debt?

That's the first thing we sort — it's Phase 2, and for many families it's worth thousands.

How fast do we get the plan?

Ledger + scenarios within 5 business days of complete documents.

Kit or Plan — which one?

The Kit is the same system as a do-it-yourself workbook: templates, trap checklist, house rules. The Plan is us building it for you — verified ledger, pile sort, leak list, three modeled speeds, and the delivery session.

What happens at the monthly review?

Fifteen minutes: actual balances against your model's schedule. Ahead, on, or behind — we adjust calmly and re-model when life changes. Milestones get marked on schedule, and removals get verified on a re-pull.

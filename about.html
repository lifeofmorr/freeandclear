About

Built on a real household first.

Free & Clear wasn't designed in a workshop. The Debt Freedom System was built live, document by document, on its founding household — and only packaged for other families after it worked there.

The founding household

Two incomes, $186K earned — and $220K owed across 21 lines once everything was finally counted. Their own list of debts missed 29 small payment plans and an entire credit card. Over $53,000 sitting on their credit reports turned out to be money they didn't owe at all: an authorized-user account that belonged to someone else, and old charge-offs being removed.

With everything on one page, they sorted the piles, plugged the leaks, and picked their speed: one steady monthly payment, debt-free in 28 months, roughly $19,600 in interest instead of the $70,000+ the minimums-only path was quietly charging — then the same payment flips into a $75,000 war chest for buying a business. That household is household #1 in our own system, running the same plan we hand to every family after them.

Who runs it

Free & Clear is a service of Lifeofmorr LLC, based in Fort Lauderdale, Florida. Lifeofmorr builds and operates online businesses; Free & Clear is the one built from its founders' own kitchen table. Operations run on a simple, deliberate stack — a documented system, a household simulator that models every plan month by month, and a client record for every family — so nothing about your plan lives in someone's memory or inbox.

What we are — and aren't

  • We are planning and budgeting education: a ledger, a pile sort, a leak list, a modeled plan, and a monthly rhythm to run it.
  • We are not licensed financial, tax, or legal advisors — and we don't sell credit repair, debt settlement, or negotiation.
  • We never touch a client's money, make their payments, contact their creditors, or promise a credit score.
  • Every number in a client plan traces to the family's documents or is labeled an estimate. No invented results, no borrowed testimonials.

Why "Free & Clear"

"Free and clear" is the property idiom for owning something outright, with no liens. That's the whole arc of the system: first the household, then — from business cash flow — the assets after it. See it all. Pay it off. Own what's next.
